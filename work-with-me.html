Work With Me

Here's how we can work together.

Each engagement is different. The underlying move is the same: find what's already working, clear what's in the way, and build from there.

Advisory — Growth Participation

For businesses ready to go from $100K to $200K in profit — or more.

$10,000 retainer against growth · 10% participation on the upside

The model is simple: I participate in your growth. The $10,000 starting retainer is credited against a 10% share of the profit increase we generate together. If we grow your business from $100K to $200K in profit, the participation is $10,000. The retainer covers it. Everything above that is shared at the same rate.

This is not a monthly coaching fee. It is an aligned stake. My outcome and yours are the same outcome.

I stay in the room while you make the real decisions — the ones about pricing, positioning, client selection, offers, and the leverage points most owners are sitting on without knowing it. There's no curriculum. No modules. One sustained conversation with someone who has seen this pattern before and can name what you're standing on.

If your current profit is under $100K, CTCSP is the right starting point. We can revisit advisory when the numbers justify the structure — or design a higher-participation arrangement if you can budget it.

Facilitation & Presentation Development

For trainers, educators, and leaders who need to deliver something that changes the room.

Not slide-deck polish. The architecture of what you're trying to do. There's a difference between a presentation that conveys information and one that shifts how someone thinks. I work on the second kind.

This is for professionals who have something important to say and are ready for it to land, transfer, and hold.

Course Development

For people building something worth finishing.

Most online courses lose participants by the third module. The reason is almost always structural: the course was designed around what the creator wanted to teach, not around what the learner needs to experience.

I've built curriculum that holds. I know what makes people quit halfway through — and what doesn't. If you're building something that teaches, I'll work with you until it earns its finish.
