Questions About Working Together (FAQ)

What is the $10,000 retainer for?

The $10,000 is a starting retainer credited against a 10% share of the profit growth generated together — it's not a flat fee, it's an advance against participation. If profit grows from $100K to $200K, the participation is $10,000 and the retainer covers it. Growth beyond that is shared at the same 10% rate.

The point of the structure is alignment: Brian Oney's outcome and your outcome are the same outcome. He's paid to grow the business, not to fill a calendar.

What if my profits are under $100K?

If current profit is under $100K, CTCSP (Cracking the Code to Success & Profits) is the right starting point — a weekly group program Brian Oney co-hosts with Scott Sylvan Bell, built for the phase that comes before one-to-one advisory.

A higher-participation arrangement may also be possible if you can budget for it. Write to brian@melioristgroup.com with a line or two about your business and we'll figure out what makes sense.

What does "no curriculum" actually mean?

There are no modules, no worksheets, and no assigned readings. Brian Oney's advisory work is one sustained conversation — over months — with someone who has seen the patterns before and can name what you are standing on.

The work emerges from your actual situation, drawn from the Meliorism 2.0 method: find what's already working, clear what's in the way, and use it deliberately. A predetermined sequence would get in the way of that.

What is the difference between the advisory work and CTCSP?

CTCSP is a weekly group program — implementation-focused, with a cohort, co-hosted with Scott Sylvan Bell. It's the right environment for building the foundation: sales psychology, influence, preeminence, and moving from knowledge to action. It's the recommended starting point for businesses under $100K in profit.

Brian Oney's one-to-one advisory is for owners who already have that foundation and are ready to scale it — with an advisor whose compensation is tied to the result.

Why work with Brian Oney specifically?

Brian Oney is the developer of Meliorism 2.0 and brings a method most advisors don't: instead of importing a framework, he finds the logic already working in your business and stands beside you while you use it deliberately.

His training in Symbolic Interactionism and his years of advisory and CTCSP work mean he reads a business in context. And because he's compensated on growth rather than on a flat fee, his incentive is structurally aligned with yours — he only does well when you do.
