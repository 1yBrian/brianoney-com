About

Hi. I'm Brian Oney.

San Francisco. Father. Advisor. Writer. I've spent a career in rooms where smart people couldn't see what they already had. That pattern became a practice.

I hold a BA in Sociology from the University of California, Santa Cruz, and a Master of Public Administration from Syracuse University. My intellectual foundation is Symbolic Interactionism — the tradition that says meaning is made in interaction, not imposed from outside. Jane Addams, the Chicago School, the idea that you can't understand a person or an organization apart from the context they're embedded in. That frame lives in everything I do.

When I sit with a business owner, I'm not looking for what's missing. I'm looking at what's already happening and naming it — often for the first time.

The Work

I do advisory work with founders and owners — one-to-one, sustained, over months. I co-host Cracking the Code to Success & Profits (CTCSP) with Scott Sylvan Bell, a weekly program for business owners with real IP and real teams. I build and deliver training and course curriculum. I write.

I founded Meliorist Group to house the advisory and training work — and to give the philosophy a practical home.

I also run Meliorism 2.0 — a daily briefing for trainers, coaches, and educators navigating an AI-shaped economy. Donation-based. The thinking made public.

The Beliefs

I believe you have intrinsic worth.

I believe you have hidden value worthy of being discovered.

I've witnessed how society, circumstances, systems and structures diminish, restrain, and distract from growing, thriving, and succeeding — in life, business, profits, joy, relationships, wisdom, wellness, and wonder.

The philosophy I work from — Meliorism 2.0 — is an extension of a simple bet: that the better version of a person, a business, or a relationship is already present. Strategy is precision removal of what conceals it.

This is not optimism. Optimism predicts improvement. Meliorism holds that improvement is available, recognizable, and the responsibility of those who can see it.

I want to discover with others how humans can overcome the resistance in the world — in systems, circumstances, and structures — to reveal and align our best selves with thriving, flourishing lives.
I do not build better businesses. I find the one that is already there.

What does "Symbolic Interactionism" mean in practice?

For Brian Oney, it means he does not arrive with a predetermined framework. He reads what is actually happening — the interactions, the patterns, the meaning people have already made — and names it, often for the first time. The insight was always there; the naming is the work.

This is the analytic engine beneath Meliorism 2.0. It is why his advisory work begins with seeing a business clearly rather than prescribing a generic strategy: meaning, leverage, and the path forward are already present in the context, waiting to be named.
