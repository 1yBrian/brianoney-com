Meliorism 2.0

A daily briefing for people who teach, train, and lead — in an economy shaped by AI.

Donation-based. Signal over noise. Published at meliorism2.com.

What it is

Meliorism 2.0 is a daily briefing for trainers, coaches, educators, and knowledge workers who need to stay oriented in an AI-shaped economy — without spending two hours reading everything to find the three things that matter.

Each issue surfaces what's relevant, names what it means for people whose work depends on the parts of human practice machines cannot replicate, and gets out of the way.

No motivational padding. No product pitches. Signal.

Who it's for

Trainers and facilitators who need to understand AI's effects on learning and behavior change — without becoming AI engineers.

Coaches and advisors who want to speak intelligently to clients about what's shifting — and what isn't.

Educators who are watching their field reorganize in real time and need a clear frame for what to hold onto and what to release.

Anyone whose work is human-centered and wants to stay that way.

Why it exists

The noise-to-signal ratio in AI coverage is brutal. Most of it is written for engineers, investors, or people who want to feel anxious. Very little of it is written for the trainer who needs to know what this means for their next cohort, or the coach who needs to advise a client on whether to automate part of their practice.

Meliorism 2.0 exists to close that gap. It's the briefing I needed and couldn't find — so I built it.

The parts of human practice that machines cannot replicate are the ones worth investing in.

Meliorism 2.0 is written and published daily by Brian Oney — advisor, trainer, and developer of the Meliorism 2.0 philosophy. It's a briefing for trainers, coaches, educators, and knowledge workers navigating an AI-shaped economy, published at meliorism2.com.

Why is Meliorism 2.0 donation-based and not free?

Brian Oney publishes Meliorism 2.0 as donation-based because producing something daily — that's actually worth reading — takes real work. "Free" implies the work costs nothing. It doesn't. Read it, and if it earns your support, support it. No paywalls on the briefings. No pressure. Just the option to sustain what you find valuable.

What makes Meliorism 2.0 different from other AI newsletters?

Meliorism 2.0 is written for people whose work is human-centered — trainers, coaches, and educators — not for engineers, investors, or people who want to feel anxious. Most AI coverage assumes the reader wants to build with AI or profit from it. Brian Oney writes for the reader who wants to understand what AI means for the irreplaceable parts of their practice.
