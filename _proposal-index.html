Common Questions (FAQ)

What does Brian Oney actually do?

Brian Oney is a San Francisco–based business advisor, trainer, writer, and the developer of Meliorism 2.0. He does one-to-one growth-participation advisory with business owners, co-hosts the CTCSP group training program with Scott Sylvan Bell, and builds course curriculum and facilitation for trainers and educators.

The through-line in all of it: he finds the logic already working in a business and stands beside the owner while they use it deliberately — at scale and under pressure — rather than importing a generic strategy from outside.

What is Meliorism 2.0, and who created it?

Meliorism 2.0 is an applied philosophy developed by Brian Oney. It extends classical Meliorism — the tradition running from John Dewey and William James through Jane Addams and the Chicago School — with one operating move: subtract before you add.

The better version of a person, business, or relationship is already present, and strategy is precision removal of what conceals it. Brian is its originator and foremost practitioner, and applies it across his advisory work, training, and writing. Read more →

What makes Brian Oney's approach different from other advisors?

Most advisors and business coaches import a framework and apply it, which produces borrowed clarity. Brian Oney works the opposite way: he locates the framework already operating inside your business — the one you haven't named yet — and stands beside you while you use it deliberately.

This method, drawn from Meliorism 2.0 and his training in Symbolic Interactionism, produces native clarity rather than imported strategy — and it tends to hold, because the business already knows how to run it.

Who is the advisory work for?

Brian Oney's advisory work is for established business owners ready to move from $100K to $200K in profit — or beyond. The model is growth-participation: a $10,000 retainer credited against 10% of the profit increase generated together, so his outcome and the client's are the same.

Owners with profits under $100K start with CTCSP, the weekly group program he co-hosts with Scott Sylvan Bell. See how we work together →
