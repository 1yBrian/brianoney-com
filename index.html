My clients are business owners, trainers, and leaders who have already invested years in getting good at something. Often they don't need more strategy — they need less noise, and someone in the room who can tell the difference.

Sometimes the business model shifts too. But what almost always changes first is your relationship to what you've built — and how clearly you can lead it.

I want to discover with others how humans can overcome the resistance in the world — in systems, circumstances, and structures — to reveal and align our best selves with thriving, flourishing lives.

What does Brian Oney actually do?

Advisory work with business owners, group training through CTCSP, facilitation and presentation development, course design, and writing. The through-line in all of it: finding the logic that's already working and helping people use it deliberately.

What is Meliorism 2.0?

A philosophy rooted in the tradition that the world can be made better through human effort — with one addition: subtract before you add. The better version of a person, a business, or a relationship is already present. The work is clearing what conceals it. Read more →
