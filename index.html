What is Meliorism 2.0, and who created it?

Meliorism 2.0 is an applied philosophy developed by Brian Oney. It extends classical Meliorism — the tradition running from John Dewey and William James through Jane Addams and the Chicago School — with one operating move: subtract before you add.

The better version of a person, business, or relationship is already present, and strategy is precision removal of what conceals it. Brian is its originator and foremost practitioner. Read more →

What makes Brian Oney's approach different from other advisors?

Most advisors import a framework and apply it, which produces borrowed clarity. Brian Oney works the opposite way: he locates the framework already operating inside your business — the one you haven't named yet — and stands beside you while you use it deliberately.

This method, drawn from Meliorism 2.0 and his training in Symbolic Interactionism, produces native clarity rather than imported strategy.
